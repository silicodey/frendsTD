# London class: concept briefs (duel units)

Art-direction prompts for ChatGPT image generation, one per message. Upload each result back to Claude, who rebuilds it in
code (`src/render/towerModels.ts`). The images are reference only: they are not shipped with the game.

**What to attach:** for **Flare** (first), attach only the **Core concept**. For the other five, attach the **Core concept**
and your approved **Flare** sheet, so the whole class matches. Do not attach the Tokyo sheets: London has its own colours.

| Unit | Rarity | Role in duels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flare | Common · Damage | focused beam that heats up on one target, melts shields |
| Photon Bloom | Rare · Damage | lobs a blooming seed of light: splash and slow |
| Halo | Epic · Support | slow field: enemies in range crawl |
| Spectra | Epic · Signature | splits a refracted beam across three targets |
| Zenith | Legendary · Signature | thinks, then fires a line of pure light through everything |
| Binary Suns | Legendary · Signature | two orbiting stars, two solar lances that set enemies ablaze |

## Flare

```text
Use the attached image as the style reference (materials, finish, level of detail). This tower belongs to a DIFFERENT class with its own colours (below), so keep only the style. Create a CONCEPT MODEL SHEET for a tower in a 3D mobile tower-defense game. Original design: it must not resemble any existing game, franchise or brand.

SUBJECT: "Flare", the entry-level beam tower of the London class (common rarity). A compact optical turret on a low hexagonal plinth: a short focusing barrel holding one faceted crystal lens at its front, carried on a small two-armed yoke. It fires a single focused beam that grows hotter the longer it holds a target and melts shields. Feeling: precise, clean, a little scientific. Simple and small: the class baseline, about as tall as the plinth is wide.

STYLE: premium stylized hard-surface sci-fi with crystal optics, clean bevels, pale satin steel and deep navy metal, faceted glass-like crystal parts, small precise emissive details. Matte, readable, calm. NOT busy, NOT glowing everywhere.

PALETTE (stick to these): body pale steel #B9C6EA; dark navy panels #18203A; cobalt accents #4A62C8; silver-blue trim #AEBCDF; emissive glow electric blue #3D7DFF; soft glow violet #A855FF; crystals may be translucent pale blue.

CONSTRUCTION (it will be rebuilt from simple 3D shapes): only simple solids — cylinders, spheres, rings/tori, octahedra and faceted crystals, bevelled boxes, flat extruded plates, lathe (turned) profiles. No organic sculpting, faces, creatures, text, numbers, logos or loose cables. Keep the very top clear (a small rank badge floats near it). Must stay readable when shown ~60 pixels tall on a phone, seen from above at 35°, among many other towers.

MOVING PARTS: 2 parts that animate (e.g. the yoke turns to aim, a focusing ring around the barrel slowly rotates). Mark each one.

SHEET LAYOUT, flat neutral mid-grey background, even studio lighting, no environment: 1) large hero view, 3/4 from above (~35° down); 2) front orthographic view; 3) top orthographic view; 4) small callout of the moving parts with arrows; 5) palette swatches. Labels for the views only.
```
